A late Virtual Safety Car threw Zandvoort's strategy calculus into disarray, but Max Verstappen's clean getaway from pole meant the home crowd never had to hold its breath for long at the front.

## Key moments

- **Lights out:** Verstappen converted pole cleanly; Norris held second through Tarzan under pressure from Piastri.
- **The VSC:** A stalled car at the Turn 3 exit brought out a Virtual Safety Car on lap 41, compressing the pit window and forcing McLaren into a split call between its two drivers.
- **Late drama:** Piastri's undercut attempt on Norris fell 1.4 seconds short at the flag — enough to hold station but not enough to change the podium order.

## Standings shift

Verstappen closes to within 23 points of the championship lead heading into the summer's second half, with McLaren's constructors' cushion narrowing for the first time in six rounds.
