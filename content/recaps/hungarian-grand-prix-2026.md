Ferrari's low-speed downforce package finally clicked into place at a circuit that's punished it for two seasons running, with Charles Leclerc controlling the race from lights to flag.

## Key moments

- **Clean start:** Leclerc held the lead into Turn 1; Hamilton jumped Russell for third at the same corner.
- **Track position over pace:** With degradation unusually low, the one-stop held up for the entire top six — nobody who pitted twice recovered the positions they lost.
- **Antonelli's day:** A disciplined defensive stint against Russell in the closing laps secured Mercedes' rookie a maiden podium.

## Standings shift

Ferrari's one-two closes the constructors' gap to McLaren to single digits for the first time this season.
