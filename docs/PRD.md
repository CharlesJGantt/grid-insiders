# Project PRD: GridInsiders (Forensic F1 Analysis Platform)

**Date:** August 31, 2026
**Project Vision:** To democratize elite-level Formula 1 engineering through a high-performance, telemetry-driven "Race Engineer" interface.

---

## 1. Executive Summary
GridInsiders is a technical analysis platform designed for "hardcore" Formula 1 enthusiasts. Unlike traditional journalism, it prioritizes forensic data visualization, performance impact analysis, and technical literacy. The platform utilizes a "Race Engineer" aesthetic—high-density, dark-mode, and data-rich—to provide insights into aerodynamics, telemetry, and car development.

## 2. Target Audience
- **The Technical Enthusiast:** Fans who prioritize engineering specs and telemetry over gossip.
- **The Data Analyst:** Users who enjoy comparing sector deltas and aero-balance reports.
- **The Aspiring Engineer:** Learners seeking to understand complex fluid dynamics and mechanical systems via the "Aero Academy."

## 3. Design System & Brand Identity
- **Name:** GridInsiders
- **Visual Language:** "Race Engineer Interface" (Technical, High-Density, Functional).
- **Core Palette (Carbon & Velocity):**
  - Surface: `#121414` (Deep Carbon)
  - Primary: `#c8c6c5` (Machine Silver)
  - Secondary/Action: `#ff571a` (Ignition Orange)
  - Tertiary/Status: `#00dbe7` (Data Cyan)
- **Typography:**
  - Headlines: Inter (Bold/Black)
  - Data/Status: JetBrains Mono (Monospaced for telemetry alignment)

## 4. Functional Pillars & Key Modules

### A. Forensic Telemetry Hub (Performance Delta Hub)
- **Dynamic Stability Matrix:** Interactive radar charts visualizing car balance and drivability.
- **Aero-Load Heatmaps:** Visual pressure distribution across front/rear wing profiles.
- **Sector Delta Components:** High-precision timing comparisons with millisecond resolution.

### B. Technical Knowledge Hub (Aero Academy)
- **Impact-Based Learning:** Moving from "Parts" to "Performance Impact" (e.g., how a floor edge change affects aero-balance).
- **Reference Archive:** Downloadable technical specs and PDF/XLSX telemetry models.

### C. Audit Trail (Season Development Log)
- **Versioned Upgrades:** Tracking car development via version codes (e.g., `UPGRADE_v2.4`).
- **Status Protocols:** Standardized system status badges (Nominal, Stress, Evaluated, Superseded).

### D. Race Operations (Race Weekend Hub)
- **Live Classification:** Real-time gap charts and tire degradation visualizations.
- **Technical Intelligence:** Deep dives into rival team secrets (e.g., "Red Bull's Floor Secret").

## 5. Information Architecture
- **Primary Navigation:** Delta Hub, Aero Academy, Telemetry, Audit Trail.
- **Secondary/Legal:** Search & Archive, Support the Grid (Donation module), Data Disclosure & Privacy.

## 6. Technical Requirements
- **Responsive Parity:** Full mobile-first optimization for all forensic widgets, ensuring complex charts collapse logically for narrow viewports.
- **Compliance:** Integrated cookie and data transparency protocols using icon-based "Authority Action" patterns.
- **Monetization:** Recurring "Support the Grid" modules integrated into article footers.
